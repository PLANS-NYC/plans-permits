Common questions

Does my renovation need a permit?

Moving plumbing, gas or walls almost always does. Cosmetic work like paint and floors usually does not. Tell us your plans and we will say which.

My board wants an architect. Is that you?

Yes. We prepare what the board and managing agent ask for alongside the DOB filing.

Who files with the City?

We do, in DOB NOW. You sign a few forms; we tell you exactly which ones and when.

Common questions

Can my basement apartment be legalized?

Sometimes. It turns on ceiling height, light and air, exits and zoning. The records study answers it for your building.

What happens to the fine?

Clearing the condition on the record is what stops the clock. We tell you what has to happen and in what order.

Will this hold up my sale?

Open violations and unpermitted work often do. Starting early gives your buyer a clear path.
